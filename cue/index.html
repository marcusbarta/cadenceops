Everything the floor's sound needs

Built around how a venue's crowd actually shifts through a shift — not a static playlist left running all day.

Crowd-level switching

Tag playlists as quiet, moderate, busy, or packed — Cue swaps the music to match as the room fills up or empties out.

Time-slot tagging

Lunch, afternoon, and dinner playlists queue themselves up on schedule, so the vibe shifts with the day automatically.

Spotify device sync

Connects straight to your venue's Spotify account and plays to whichever speaker or device is active on the floor.

Playlist generation

Describe the vibe you want and Cue builds and analyses a playlist to match, ready to tag and slot into the schedule.

PIN-protected, zero setup per shift

One shared login for the floor — nothing to configure device by device before service starts.

Manual override, anytime

Staff can override the current playlist or crowd level directly when the automatic pick isn't quite right.
