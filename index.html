Why Cadence

A good shift has a rhythm — bookings, prep, seating, turnover, all in step. Cadence Ops software is built to keep that rhythm smooth, not fight against it.

Most tools are built for a slow Tuesday and hope the Friday rush doesn't notice. We build for the rush first — for the exact minute a table turns, a shift handoff happens, or last call hits — and let everything calmer than that take care of itself.
